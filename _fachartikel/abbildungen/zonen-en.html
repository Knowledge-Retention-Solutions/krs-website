Three zones of company knowledge

What stays when someone leaves, and what leaves with them

Zone 1
Documented
  • Manuals
  • Standards
  • Drawings
Available and findable
Zone 2
Scattered
  • Emails
  • Project folders
  • Legacy systems
There, but hard to find
Zone 3
In people's heads
  • Why this material gets tighter tolerances
  • How you hear a bearing failing early
  • Which orders to turn down
Leaves with the person
Search tools and AI can open it up
